Your Leaders

Our District Board

The Missouri-Arkansas District Board leads the district. Board members are elected annually at DCON and serve throughout the year.

The district board is responsible for leading all clubs in the district. Board meetings are held throughout the service year where members discuss the district bylaws, work in committees, and plan the District Convention. For more information about DCON, visit the DCON page.
